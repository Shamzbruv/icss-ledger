Partnership Contract Library

Build structured partnership and revenue-share agreements for people contributing to HaloManage. Choose the role, set the commercial terms, and generate a detailed contract draft ready to copy into the ICSS contract-signing workflow.

These templates distinguish a revenue-share commercial collaborator from a formal general partner. That distinction matters because carrying on business together with a view to profit can create partnership consequences under Jamaican law. The formal-partnership option should be selected only when legal ownership/partner status is actually intended.

Revenue-share formula

Partner share = agreed percentage × Net Distributable Revenue. Net Distributable Revenue means collected HaloManage revenue inside the selected scope, less legitimate and documented business expenses defined in the contract.

Be specific: all HaloManage revenue, partner-sourced clients only, a territory, a portfolio, or another defined pool.
The formal option adds capital, management, profit/loss, withdrawal and dissolution terms. It is not the default.
