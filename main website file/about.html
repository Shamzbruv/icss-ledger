Jamaican roots. Global ambition.

From a creative spark to practical digital solutions.

A story of creativity, curiosity and building something useful.

iCreate Solutions & Services began with an idea long before it became a registered business. In 2023, Shamar Baker imagined the iCreate name while exploring animation as a hobby and taking on graphic design work.

While working at Conduent, Shamar was introduced to coding. The possibilities caught his attention, and building his first website turned that curiosity into a new direction. Collaborating with developers across the world and contributing to a range of projects helped shape both his skills and his approach to solving problems.

The business name was officially registered in July 2025. Today, iCreate Solutions & Services builds websites, mobile apps and business systems for Jamaican businesses and clients around the world.

Along the way, ICSS created Grace Connect, a Christian community and church platform born in Jamaica, and ICSS Bookings, a booking system for people who want a professional website experience within a smaller budget.

The ambition is straightforward: develop useful solutions that fit how businesses actually work, make local technology easier to access, and help Jamaican talent serve the world.
